## Governance

- Backup material may propose Factors, sources, thresholds or rules.
- Backup material is not automatically part of the Evidence Registry.
- Every deployable rule still requires resolvable primary evidence, scope review, human approval and versioning.
- Superseded materials remain preserved unless an explicit cleanup decision is made.
