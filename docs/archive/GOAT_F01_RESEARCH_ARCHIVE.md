Goat Grazing/Browsing was removed from the active RangeMatch MVP to reduce scope. This file preserves prior F01 research provenance only. Its sources, hypotheses, and conclusions must not be loaded by the runtime, referenced by an active Operation Profile, or included in the Mireye submission package.

Archived source IDs: `SRC_F01_007`, `SRC_F01_008`, `SRC_F01_009`.

Archived decision: U.S. Extension guidance suggested that goats may be better adapted than cattle to some steep or rocky settings, but no sufficiently applicable U.S. primary resource-selection study was identified. Two non-U.S. domestic-goat GPS studies showed context-dependent terrain effects and did not justify an automatic steep-terrain benefit. The proposed relationship remained unverified when the Profile was deferred.

Reactivation requires a new scope decision, restoration into the active registries, source review, variable review, deterministic-rule review, and human approval.
